\# HR Attrition Analytics Dashboard — Power BI



An interactive \*\*HR Analytics dashboard developed in Microsoft Power BI\*\* to analyze historical employee attrition patterns across workforce demographics, compensation, tenure, job roles, departments, education, and business travel.



\## 📊 Dashboard Preview



!\[HR Attrition Analytics Dashboard](images/dashboard\_overview.png)



\## 🎯 Project Objective



The objective of this project is to transform employee workforce data into an interactive HR dashboard that enables users to explore historical attrition patterns and identify workforce segments associated with higher employee turnover.



The dashboard focuses on descriptive and diagnostic HR analytics rather than predictive modeling.



\## 📌 Dashboard KPIs



The dashboard provides an overview of key workforce indicators including:



\- Total Employee Count

\- Total Attrition

\- Overall Attrition Rate

\- Average Employee Age

\- Average Salary

\- Average Employee Tenure



\## 🔍 Analysis Areas



The dashboard analyzes attrition across:



\- Department

\- Job Role

\- Age

\- Gender

\- Education

\- Salary Slab

\- Years at Company

\- Marital Status

\- Business Travel



Interactive slicers allow users to filter the dashboard by:



\- Age Group

\- Business Travel

\- Department

\- Education Field



\## 💡 Key Insights



The dashboard enables HR users to identify:



\- Departments and job roles contributing most to historical attrition

\- Attrition patterns across employee age groups

\- Differences in attrition across salary segments

\- Workforce turnover patterns by employee tenure

\- Attrition distribution across education and demographic groups

\- Potential relationships between business travel and employee attrition



These findings represent historical patterns in the dataset and should not be interpreted as causal relationships.
